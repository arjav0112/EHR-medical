  va_dod_suicide_2024.pdf  https://www.healthquality.va.gov/guidelines/MH/srb/VADoD-CPG-Suicide-Risk-Full-CPG-2024_Final_508.pdf

All files are freely available from public government and professional association sources.
The system works without them — agents fall back to Gemini Flash clinical knowledge.
